# Fonts

Everything here is served by the app itself; nothing is fetched from the internet.

- `BoekSerif-Roman.woff2`, `BoekSerif-Italic.woff2`: Adobe's Source Serif 4 variable fonts (release 4.005,
  https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-serif), subset to Latin and Latin Extended-A with every OpenType feature
  kept. The SIL Open Font License reserves the name "Source" for unmodified fonts, so the subset is called
  Boek Serif. Licence: `OFL-SourceSerif4.md`. Used for titles and for text you read (the book, the script).
- `InstrumentSans.woff2`: Instrument Sans variable (weights 400 to 700, Latin), from the Fontsource package
  `@fontsource-variable/instrument-sans` 5.3.0. Licence: `OFL-InstrumentSans.txt`. Used for everything you click.
